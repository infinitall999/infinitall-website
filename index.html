About the Founder

Twenty years
of creation in
impossible places

Naz is the founder of Infinitall. She is a leader, strategist, coach, and systems thinker whose career has lived at the intersection of social entrepreneurship and development, in order to shift collective consciousness to abundance and impact.

She gradated from Harvard University, consulted for the World Bank, worked as a Senior Research Fellow at Annenberg Social Innovation Lab, trained hundreds of founders and community leaders across multiple countries, and led social entrepreneurship initiatives in Latin America, Africa, Europe, Middle East and now, in the United State.

Alongside her professional work, she has explored inner transformation, heal-making and expanded awareness through love for all. These experiences reshaped her understanding of creativity, leadership, and what it truly takes to build something that lasts.

Infinitall means Infinity for All. She believes everyone has access to infinity.

Our Philosophy

A Miracle Path
in building

Most ventures stall not because the idea is wrong, but because the system holding it is incomplete. Business strategy, social impact, and founder alignment - the three pillars- are treated as trade-offs. We treat them as one architecture.

At Infinitall, we call this Making Miracles. Not as metaphor, but as method. The miracle is what happens when all three pillars of your venture move together. You are happy and in peace, money comes and impact being made. You are happy and in peace, money comes and impact being made.
